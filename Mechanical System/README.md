# EASE Exoskeleton – Mechanical System
The Mechanical System can be further divided into the **Exoskeleton's Docking Station** and **Exoskeleton's Back and Arm rests**. The docking station was made on Fusion360 whilst (majority of) the back and arm rest was made on Solidworks.

## ⚙ Docking Station
### Description
The docking station consists of 3D printed parts custom-designed to the current backrest and is held by a frame made out of 40 x 40 20 series V-slot extrusions. It is design for easy use so the elderly walks forwards into it.
Please see the video to demonstrate how it works.

The following is a list of all files and descriptions:

`ASSEMBLY - Full Assembly` - This is an full exoskeleton assembly with the original leg assembly but uses an outdated back and arm rest assembly.

`Armrest motor mount` - The current adaptor between the original leg design and the arm rests, however, this has been prone to shearing at a 15% infill.

`Docking Station Assembly` - A one sided assembly of the Docking Station showing the location of armrest, hinge, stopping mechanism and shape of frame.

`Docking Station Arm` - Arms that extend from the under the armrest to the backrest to support the backrest weight and ensure the exoskeleton's U-joint does not rotate and cause the exoskeleton to fall off. Has a section cut off from it for the weight based locking mechanism. 
Two springs are to be situate in the small circle cut outs and when exoskeleton rest upon it, the piece's extrusion at the bottom hits the stopper, preventing the hinge to return to its original position.

`Ramp fitting` - Ramp to stop the hinge from overturning.

`Returning Hinge` - Inaccurate CAD of TruClose® Self-Closing Pool Gate Safety Hinges but with joint added for demonstration.

`Spring Stopper` - Attachment to pole that works with the spring system on the `Docking Station Arm`s to stop the hinge from returning when exoskeleton is on it.

`Vslot Corner joint` - The joint that is used to construct the Vslot frame.
### Recommended improvements
- Redesign or reprint a stronger `armrest motor mount`
- Stronger joint design to restict the frame's vertical poles more
- Design stopping mechanism so the hinge does not over or under turn when returning to original position, currently the ramp has this job but needs constant tuning
- Design an physical indicator when the elderly should stop walking into the docking station and unstrap
- Current design has a 20 x 40 Rectangular Pole running in the middle - a tripping hazard for elderly

#💪 Back and Arm rests

### Description
The Back and Arm rests is a complicated system featuring multiple 3D printed components and a U joint to cater for the flexibility of a humans' hip joint.

The following is a list of all files and descriptions:

'Armrest Front Section L and R` - The front section of the armrest with cut out for potentiometer and emergency stop. Also potentiometer knob and visual dial.

### Recommended improvements
- Add padding on the backrests for more comfort
- Better strap design as current design has some slipping
- Better design so exoskeleton legs are closer to elderly's legs
# General advice
- Have a good plan at the start of every term to keep track of progress and outline dependancies
- Have a agreed method of sharing files (like using this repositry or through Fusion's shared project spaces)
- Good communication between design teams so integration is easy
- Design with manufacturing process in mind (size of design, assembly, fasteners, manufacturing time)
